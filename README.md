# Paytm-WarrantyVault


Paytm WarrantyVault : A Blockchain-backed Warranty Protocol

Our idea was simple:
Today, warranties and invoices are fragmented across paper receipts, emails, and brand portals. Customers lose proofs of purchase, warranty claims become painful, and ownership transfer during resale is messy.

We built a protocol where:
- vendors can activate warranties in one click after a Paytm transaction,
- AI extracts invoice/product details automatically using OCR,
- warranties become tokenized digital ownership credentials stored on-chain,
- and ownership/warranty can seamlessly transfer during resale.

We felt this could genuinely benefit the Paytm ecosystem by turning transaction data into a trusted proof-of-ownership infrastructure while improving customer trust and post-purchase experience.
